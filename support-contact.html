HARDWARE ASSURANCE

Warranty Information & Coverage

Every device sold on Gadget Galaxy carries an inclusive 24-month manufacturer and retailer warranty backed by certified technicians.

What Is Covered
  • • Internal motherboard, processor, or silicon architecture failure.
  • • Touchscreen digitizer and OLED pixel anomalies under normal use.
  • • Internal speaker, microphone, or wireless antenna malfunctions.
  • • Battery capacity loss dropping below 80% in the first 24 months.
  • • Free labor and factory-original replacement parts.
What Is Excluded
  • • Accidental liquid submersion or severe drops on hard concrete.
  • • Unauthorized third-party disassembly or unapproved modifications.
  • • Normal surface cosmetic scratches and everyday paint wear.
  • • Loss or theft (Coverable under optional Gadget Care Elite).
